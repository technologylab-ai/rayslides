## QR Code generator library

Crowdplay vendors the C implementation of Project Nayuki's QR Code generator,
version 1.8.0, under the MIT License. The complete copyright and permission
notice is preserved in `src/qr/qrcodegen.c` and `src/qr/qrcodegen.h`.

Source: <https://github.com/nayuki/QR-Code-generator/tree/v1.8.0/c>

## MPack

Builds with `-Dneovim=true` compile MPack version 1.1.1, Copyright (c)
2015-2021 Nicholas Fraser and the MPack authors, under the MIT License. The
complete copyright and permission notice is included in the pinned dependency
archive and installed as `share/rayslides/licenses/MPack-LICENSE.txt` (or the
equivalent macOS app resource). Builds with Neovim support disabled do not
fetch, compile, or install MPack.

Source: <https://github.com/ludocode/mpack/tree/v1.1.1>

## JetBrains Mono

Builds with `-Dneovim=true` bundle JetBrains Mono Regular version 2.304,
Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font
